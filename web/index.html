Where your 40 GB of wasted disk space actually goes

Tidy Mac zeroes in on the junk files that build up quietly in the background.

📦

Old Installers & Archives

Every time you download an app, its .dmg, .pkg, or .zip sits in Downloads taking up 2 GB each. Tidy Mac flags installers older than 7 days for quick review.

🧹

Hidden App Leftovers

Dragging an app to the Trash leaves folders in ~/Library/Application Support and ~/Library/Caches. We track down orphaned bundle remnants so your Mac stays clean.

👯

Duplicate File Finder

Checks file size, partial hash, and full SHA-256 so there are zero false positives. And our safety guard guarantees that at least one original copy always stays untouched.

📂

In-Place Folder Organizer

Have 300 loose files in Downloads? Tidy Mac sorts them into Documents, Media, Projects, and Archives in-place, without creating messy nested folders or breaking project repos.

💬

Plain-English Cleanup Agent

Tell it what you want: "Clean screenshots older than a month". It creates a dry-run preview showing exactly what would move so you can review before anything happens.

⏰

Set-and-Forget Schedule

Set a daily, weekly, or low-disk-space schedule. Apple’s native LaunchAgent handles it quietly in the background, only running rules you have explicitly turned on.
